# Weather settings

| Value | What it does |
|---|---|
| Template | Pick clear, rain, heavy snow, blizzard ... to set clouds, precipitation, snow, fog, lightning and wind strength at once (snow depth and wind direction stay) |
| Clouds | 0-100. Clouds in the sky and how dark it is |
| Precipitation | 0-100 (one decimal). For rain the drops change too: sprinkle → drizzle → rain → downpour → storm |
| Snow | When checked, rain turns into drizzle and then into snow (and back when unchecked) |
| Snow depth (cm) | How deep the snow piles up ([Snow and snow cover](snow)) |
| Fog | 0-100. The color follows clear, rain and snow by itself. Heavy rain or snow also makes it harder to see |
| Lightning | Flashes |
| Wind direction and strength | Where rain and snow drift. Slanted rain and snow reach under the edges of roofs |

> Rain and snow hit **things with a collider** (the connector plugin "Weather × Physics").
